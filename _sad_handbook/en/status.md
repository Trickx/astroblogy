- **Early stage.**
  The script is at an early stage of development.
  Its functions, thresholds and outputs may still change.
- **Feasibility study.**
  The script is a proof of concept: it explores whether the optical system can be diagnosed reliably from the shapes of the stars alone.
  It is not a finished or validated measuring instrument.
- **Designed with AI.**
  The script and this handbook were designed with the help of AI.
- **May contain errors.**
  Computations, verdicts and explanations can be wrong.
  Check the results against your own experience before you adjust your equipment.
- **Recommendations of the AI review.**
  The AI review sometimes suggests what to do, for example an adjustment or a piece of equipment.
  These suggestions are not purchase recommendations.
  There is no guarantee that following them improves anything.
