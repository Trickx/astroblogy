The verdicts of the last Calculate, topic by topic, with suggested corrections in the order in which to work through them.

While the Assessment tab is selected, the left side of the dialog shows the assessment instead of the preview.
The Assessment tab on the right has no settings; it only says so.

## Left side: the assessment

### Script assessment
{: .display}

Filled by Calculate.
It begins with the suggested order of corrections, followed by one entry per topic (data, mount, focus, tracking, tilt, spacing, coma, field curvature ...) with its verdict - **[OK]**, **[Note]** or **[Action]** - and, where something is to be done, a suggestion after an arrow.
The settings of Setup › General - optics type, mount and guiding - shape the suggestions.
